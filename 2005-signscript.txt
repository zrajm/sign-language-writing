Grushkin, who have been advocating for the creation of a writing system for ASL
since before the turn of the millenium ([Grushkin 1998]; [Grushkin 2017]),
created the linear (or "alphabetic" in his parlance) writing system SignScript
([Grushkin 2005]).

Symbols are typically written in the order: Handshape, orientation, location,
movement. Grushkin explains how to use the system in a series of videos on
Youtube ([Grushkin 2010a], [Grushkin 2010b], [Grushkin 2010c], [Grushkin
2010d])---Unfortunately the videos are in ASL, without subtitles.)

* https://www.facebook.com/DrDonGrushkin
